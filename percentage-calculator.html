How to Use the Percentage Calculator

This tool answers three common percentage questions:

  • What is X% of Y? Example: What is 20% of 150? Answer: 30
  • X is what percent of Y? Example: 30 is what percent of 150? Answer: 20%
  • Percentage change: Example: From 100 to 150 = 50% increase

Common Percentage Formulas

X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y

X is what % of Y = (X ÷ Y) × 100

% change = ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100
